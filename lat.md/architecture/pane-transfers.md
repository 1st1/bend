# Pane transfers

Pane and tab drag operations within and between browser windows.

See [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests|Test coverage]].

## Pane drag and drop

[PaneDragging.swift](../../Sources/Browse/PaneDragging.swift) provides title-bar dragging within and between tabs, with a floating [[lat.md/architecture/pane-transfers#Dragging into a new window|thumbnail card]] and blue previews for placing a pane above, below, left, or right of another pane.

Hovering the title displays the page URL in the pane’s existing bottom URL preview, highlights its favicon and text in a compact pill, and crossfades the favicon to a four-way drag arrow over 100 ms; Reduce Motion makes the change immediate. Native tracking confines the hover treatment to the title pill. Only the title pill starts an internal pane drag after a movement threshold. Empty header space instead participates in [[lat.md/architecture/windows#Window dragging]]; toolbar buttons keep their normal actions.

[[Sources/Browse/BrowseWindow.swift#BrowseWindow#sendEvent]] resolves a visible title handle belonging to the selected tab, then passes the press through native AppKit dispatch for window activation and ordering. [[Sources/Browse/BrowseWindow.swift#PaneMouseTargetView]] supplies a stable mouse target in the native window frame; AppKit tracks this view rather than a SwiftUI title that may disappear during a transfer. It claims hit testing only during validated title presses, leaving ordinary content, toolbar, hover, and scrolling targets unchanged. The complete press/drag/release sequence reaches this target, including after sidebar hover hides the source. The window retains idle, tracking, and cancelled-awaiting-release states. Ownership clears before applying a drop, while cancellation still lets AppKit deliver the final release to the stable target. A fresh press resets ownership; closing the window clears it entirely. Pane handles install no event monitors.

The floating thumbnail card and window-level drop previews ignore hit testing. SwiftUI handle teardown explicitly cancels tracking even if AppKit still keeps the retired view mounted. Teardown, Escape, and window deactivation remove the thumbnail card and drop previews. Pointer positions are compared directly with visible pane rectangles, avoiding AppKit's pasteboard destination routing into WebKit.

[PaneDropEdge.swift](../../Sources/BrowseCore/PaneDropEdge.swift) chooses the nearest normalized edge and its half-pane preview across the target's full visible content area. Previews stay visible while the pointer moves within a target. Only target changes clear the old highlight; edge or bounds changes update its path without hiding it. Leaving valid targets, cancelling, or completing a drag clears the preview. Self-drops, stale sources, and unrelated windows are rejected. Pane-edge rearrangement is disabled while zoomed, but sidebar transfers remain available. External pasteboard drags do not initiate pane rearrangement.

Sidebar tab rows highlight during a drag; hovering for 400 ms selects the tab while retaining the original drag source. Newly mounted destination panes become targets immediately. Dropping directly on another tab atomically adds the pane to the right of its focused pane and selects it, including when the source is zoomed; dropping into empty sidebar space detaches it into a new tab at the end of the list. Dropping on the source tab is a no-op with no drop highlight. Successful sidebar transfers exit zoom. Leaving a row or cancelling clears the delayed selection and all previews.

[Session.swift](../../Sources/BrowseCore/Session.swift) removes the source leaf, collapses its former split, and inserts the same pane beside the target with an equal split. Unrelated branches retain their identities and fractions. [BrowserStore.swift](../../Sources/Browse/BrowserStore.swift) focuses the moved pane and saves the layout without closing pages or adding closed-item history. Same-tab moves retain mounted pane views and WebKit controllers. Cross-tab transfers preserve the page ID, live controller, navigation history, and extension tab identity. An emptied source tab is removed; an emptied workspace receives a blank tab. Transfers create no closed-item history and persist both layouts together. Moves animate over 180 ms unless Reduce Motion is enabled; divider dragging still updates immediately.

## Cross-window dragging

[CrossWindowDragging.swift](../../Sources/Browse/CrossWindowDragging.swift) resolves drag destinations across open Bend windows while keeping the pressed window responsible for the mouse gesture.

Pane and tab overlays follow the pointer into the destination window using screen-coordinate conversion. Visible pane edges accept splits; sidebar rows accept joins, and tab insertion bands preserve a whole tab. Empty sidebar space accepts a pane as a new tab. Hover selection uses the destination store. A valid blue pane or sidebar preview brings its destination window to the front without changing the key window, preserving the source’s captured gesture until drop. Unrelated windows, hidden targets, stale sources, and canvas drops into a zoomed pane are excluded. Escape and window deactivation clear previews without transferring ownership. Dropping outside visible Bend windows follows [[lat.md/architecture/pane-transfers#Dragging into a new window]].

[BrowserStore.swift](../../Sources/Browse/BrowserStore.swift) validates both layouts before moving controllers and rebinding their navigation, focus, link-opening, history, and extension-configuration callbacks. Existing WebViews retain documents, forms, navigation stacks, page zoom, and pinch magnification. Whole-tab moves retain the split tree, tab ID, and dedicated-link pane; moving a single pane repairs its source layout and clears an orphaned dedicated-link role. An emptied source window closes after the destination owns its live pages. The window manifest retains only the surviving windows; cancelling or rejecting a drop never closes its source. Transfers create no closed-history entries and save both sessions. Source membership is published before native views detach; retiring canvas snapshots cannot recreate transferred controllers, and detached hosts stop resizing WebViews now mounted elsewhere.

Extension adapters retain their identity and receive a window-move notification after both models reflect the new ownership. Closing the source window then releases only controllers still owned by it. [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Cross-window live transfers]] and [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Cross-window drag gestures]] cover ownership and native drag routing.

## Dragging into a new window

[[Sources/Browse/DetachedDragPreview.swift#DetachedDragPreview]] provides a floating thumbnail for pane and sidebar-tab drags, while [[Sources/Browse/BrowserWindows.swift#BrowserWindows#detach]] creates a destination window only on an outside drop.

The rounded translucent card has a favicon, title, miniature pane layout, subtle rim, and shadow. It follows the pointer in screen coordinates without taking focus or intercepting input. Its thumbnail uses the actual mounted pane frames when available, without reapplying layout minimum sizes; the card height fits that thumbnail plus its title bar and narrow border, without letterboxing, including throughout resizing. Both outer dimensions are capped at 250 points (240 in compact mode), scaling tall and wide thumbnails uniformly within the space left by the title bar and border. The title has low horizontal compression resistance, so truncation cannot impose a wider minimum window and leave space beside portrait thumbnails. Pane drags grow from a compact card to a larger preview outside visible Bend windows. Sidebar-tab drags show the thumbnail only outside the source window and restore their compact title badge on return. Over another Bend window the thumbnail uses its compact size and existing reorder/join targets remain available. Pointer movement updates the panel origin immediately. A single size-only interpolation keeps the cursor anchor fixed throughout growth, shrinkage, and reversal; it never replays an earlier pointer position. A 12-point band around window edges stabilizes the visual size, while drop eligibility still uses exact bounds. Reduce Motion removes the size transition. Live panes are never bitmap-rendered for thumbnails: native glass belongs to the window compositor and must remain untouched during a drag. The canvas draws miniature title chrome separately. [[Sources/Browse/WebSurfaceHost.swift#WebSurfaceHost#snapshotContentRect]] excludes content obscured by the header and find bar; [[Sources/Browse/DetachedDragPreview.swift#DragPreviewCanvas#updateWebSnapshot]] places asynchronous WebKit captures into their original visible pane-local rectangles. [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Drag preview glass isolation]] verifies capture isolation, header geometry, and retained page state. The canvas redraws on resizing instead of stretching a cached layer. Unavailable snapshots retain the pane layout and address without navigating hidden pages. [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Drag preview rendered proportions]] checks rendered content and edge coverage for wide, tall, and shallow panes.

[[Sources/Browse/CrossWindowDragging.swift#WindowDragTarget]] excludes preview panels from both native window lookup and app window ordering, so they cannot mask valid drop targets. New-window drops require a real screen point outside all visible Bend windows. Invalid positions inside a Bend window do not create a new window. The existing movement threshold, Escape, deactivation, source-removal, and mouse-up cleanup dismiss the card without changing ownership on cancellation.

On release, the destination is sized from the source and positioned near the pointer within the destination display's visible frame. The existing live transfer replaces its initial blank tab without creating closed history or leaving an extra blank tab. Pane drops move one page; tab drops preserve the complete split tree and tab identity. Pins, controllers, document state, and page zoom survive. An exhausted source closes only after transfer succeeds, and the window manifest saves the result. [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Detached window ownership]], [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Outside-window drag gestures]], and [[lat.md/tests/pane-transfers-tests#Drag preview motion]] verify persistence, native gestures, preview cleanup, cursor tracking, and existing-target compatibility.
