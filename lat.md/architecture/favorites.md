# Favorites

Shared favorite tabs and per-account Google destinations.

See [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests|Test coverage]].

## Sidebar favorites

[[Sources/Browse/SidebarFavorites.swift#SidebarFavorites]] places up to nine saved websites in a three-column favicon grid above the tab list, in both docked and floating sidebars.

A tab’s context menu offers Add to Favorites for HTTP(S) pages and Remove from Favorites for an associated page. For split tabs the focused pane supplies the saved address. Favoriting moves the existing tab’s sidebar representation into the grid without replacing its live pages, split layout, pin state, or selection. [[Sources/Browse/BrowserFavorites.swift#BrowserStore#sidebarTabs]] hides tabs represented by a favorite; removal returns them to the list. Grid context menus also allow closing the open tab, respecting pins, while retaining the saved favorite.

[[Sources/BrowseCore/SidebarFavorite.swift#SidebarFavorite]] saves a stable identity, original URL, title, and bounded PNG favicon in the shared profile’s session. Favorites appear in every window and remain separate between Debug and production profiles. [[Sources/BrowseCore/Session.swift#Page]] retains the favorite association through navigation, restoration, and pane/window transfers. Older sessions default to no favorites. Restored collections are capped at nine and reject invalid addresses, duplicate identities/addresses, and oversized icons.

[[Sources/Browse/BrowserFavorites.swift#BrowserStore#openFavorite]] selects the current window’s associated pane without navigating or reloading it, otherwise adopts a tab at the exact saved URL or opens a new one. Closing a favorite’s tab keeps its tile; reopening uses the original saved URL or its configured [[lat.md/architecture/favorites#Google account favorites|Google account launch URL]]. Each window can have its own live instance. Adding an already-saved URL reuses its tile and makes the clicked tab its target, leaving the previous tab available in the regular list. Split layouts remain intact when selected through a tile. [[Sources/Browse/BrowserFavorites.swift#BrowserStore#openLocation]] routes location-popup entry to a new regular tab when its destination differs from the favorite’s saved HTTP(S) origin; same-origin entries stay in place. Searches and internal pages also open separately. Ordinary page-link navigation is unchanged. [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Favorite location entry preserves the source]] covers native popup submission and source preservation.

[[Sources/Browse/BrowserFavorites.swift#BrowserStore#refreshFavoriteIcon]] snapshots only an existing controller’s bounded favicon from the saved site’s origin. Late favicon arrivals update the tile, while navigation to another origin cannot replace the saved icon. Restored tiles render saved icons or a hostname initial without creating WebViews or making independent network requests. Tiles use [[Sources/Browse/BrowserView.swift#Chrome#sidebarItemCornerRadius|the tab corner radius]]. Inactive tiles keep the same semantic fill as hovered tab rows; selected tiles share [[Sources/Browse/BrowserView.swift#TabSelectionMaterial|the active tab’s interactive glass material]], without a separate painted rim. Selection transitions respect Reduce Motion. Tooltips and accessibility labels identify the site. [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Favorite tabs and persistence]], [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Shared favorites and pane transfers]], [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Favorite favicon snapshots]], and [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Favorite tile interaction]] cover the feature.

## Google account favorites

[[Sources/Browse/FavoriteCustomization.swift#FavoriteCustomizationView]] prompts for an optional email when favoriting Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Calendar. Existing Google tiles expose Google Account in their context menu to edit the choice.

[[Sources/BrowseCore/GoogleFavorite.swift#GoogleFavoriteService]] recognizes only the exact Google service hosts and builds a canonical service-home URL with an email-valued authuser parameter. Configured launches pass that destination and an Email hint through Google’s AccountChooser endpoint. This avoids depending on numbered account paths and retains the email through signed-out redirects, including Calendar. Query encoding preserves plus-address tags. These are Google web routes rather than a versioned API contract; Google still controls authentication and account availability. [Google’s account help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977) describes signing into multiple accounts and default-account behavior.

[[Sources/Browse/FavoriteCustomization.swift#BrowserStore#saveFavoriteCustomization]] validates the email and original target before changing favorites. Cancel and invalid input leave the tab and collection unchanged. Blank input keeps the current link when adding, or restores the service home when editing. A configured email uses the service home instead of the source page’s account-number path or message/file details. Distinct emails can have separate favorites within the shared nine-tile cap; duplicate saved URLs reuse a tile on creation and cannot collide during editing. Tooltips and accessible names include the configured email.

[[Sources/BrowseCore/SidebarFavorite.swift#SidebarFavorite#launchAddress]] supplies the account-specific launch address. Saving a new account navigates an already-open target to that address; saving unchanged settings preserves its live page. Normal favorite selection retains the current live tab. Closing/reopening or restoring the browser launches through the saved email, replacing persisted numbered Google URLs whose account order may have changed. Credentials and browser sessions remain managed by Google and WebKit. [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Google favorite account URLs]], [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Google favorite customization lifecycle]], and [[lat.md/tests/favorites-tests#Google favorite popup presentation]] cover URL construction, model behavior, and native presentation; authenticated multi-account routing requires a signed-in Google session to validate.
