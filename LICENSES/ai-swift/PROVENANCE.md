# AI module provenance

The AI module in Sources/AI originated in the local ai-swift project, through commit c6abb6e. It is maintained as part of Bend, with its tests in Tests/AITests and architecture in lat.md/ai-sdk.

Bend retains its parameterless-tool normalization and regression coverage in addition to the upstream provider and agent implementation. LICENSE and NOTICE in this directory preserve the original attribution and licensing terms.
